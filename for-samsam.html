and so the rest of my life begins

my favourite view

Before you, my life was a house with the lights left off —rooms I moved through by memory and never by wonder,a kettle boiled for one, a window facing weather,days that arrived politely and left without a sound.

Then you. Not a knock — the morning itself,arriving the way spring arrives in a cold country:all at once, and everywhere, and impossible to argue with.You did not decorate my life. You furnished it.

I have learned your name the way the sea learns the shore —Samsam, Samsam — twice, because once was never enough,a word my mouth keeps returning tolike a prayer it is not finished saying.

You changed me the way rivers change mountains:slowly, entirely, and for good.I am kinder now. I am braver. I am here —and being here has never once felt like waiting.

If love is a country, I have burned the map.I have no interest in going home.Wherever you are laughing, that is the border.Wherever you are sleeping, that is the capital.

So keep the window seat. Keep the headphones,the black scarf, the small smile you give the camerawhen you think nobody is watching —I am watching. I have always been watching.

And I will still be watching when we are old,when the trains have all been given other names,and somebody asks me what I did with my one life.

I will say: I loved her.And they will say: is that all?And I will say — that was everything.

Yours, entirely
now & every version of later
